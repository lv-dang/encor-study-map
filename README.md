# ENCOR 350-401 Last-Minute Study Map

A single-page, mobile-first study tool for the Cisco **CCNP/CCIE Enterprise Core (ENCOR 350-401) v1.2** exam. It's one self-contained `index.html` file: open it in any browser, online or offline.

**Live page:** https://lv-dang.github.io/encor-study-map/

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Overview | Domain weights, an "Am I ready to pass?" panel, per-sub-objective scores, ~45 deep-dive panels with 59 diagrams (step-by-step flows for SD-WAN bring-up, SD-Access/LISP lookup, OSPF LSAs, STP, PIM-SM, PTP, IKE, NETCONF, EEM…), "what's wrong here?" exhibits and complete config samples with verify commands |
| 🃏 Trigger cards | 223 requirement → solution flip cards with spaced repetition (10 min → 1 h → 1 day → 3 days) and diagrams on the back |
| 🎯 Scenario quiz | 274 questions: single choice, choose two, ordering, matching, show-output and exhibit questions, with a "why not" reason for most wrong options. Modes: Practice, Retry wrong, Weak topics, a **timed mock exam** (100 questions weighted like the blueprint, 120 min, unseen questions first) and a **Final check** of 30 held-back questions |
| 🔢 Numbers | Ports, protocol numbers, timers, defaults, DSCP/AF grid, OSPF/EIGRP/BGP values, FHRP/multicast addresses, API paths, HTTP codes, grouped by domain and tagged with blueprint numbers |
| ⏱ Exam day | Pacing plan, must-know lines for every blueprint sub-item (1.1.a – 6.7), classic traps |
| 🔍 Search | Search across cards, quiz, panels and numbers |

Every card and question is tagged with its official v1.2 sub-objective, a source label and the matching Official Cert Guide chapter. Progress (cards, first-try quiz results, mock scores) is saved in your browser's localStorage. It stays on your device and doesn't sync between devices.

## Readiness rule

"Ready" means: in every domain, at least 80 % of the practice questions seen and at least 85 % right on the first try, **plus** at least 85 % on the 30-question Final check that never appears in practice. It's a self-check heuristic, not a pass prediction.

## Coverage

- Aligned with the official ENCOR v1.2 exam topics (Architecture 15 %, Virtualization 10 %, Infrastructure 30 %, Network Assurance 10 %, Security 20 %, Automation and AI 15 %), including Catalyst Center AI-powered workflows, PTP, SSM/bidir/MSDP and the new product names (Catalyst Center, SD-WAN Manager/Controller/Validator).
- Topics dropped in v1.2 (wireless, 802.1X/MAB/WebAuth NAC) are not included except as notes.
- Minimum per sub-objective: 5 cards, 5 quiz questions, 1 visual (4 / 3 / 1 in the 10 % domains).

## Sources

Original study notes and questions written from:

Where the books disagree (e.g. SD-WAN component names, normal VLAN range, IGMP query interval, RADIUS ports), the page shows both values. No exam dumps: all questions are original and written from the books' concepts.
